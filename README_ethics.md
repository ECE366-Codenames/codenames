# Following the ACM Code of Ethics in Our Project

1. Clause 3.02: Ensure proper and achievable goals and objectives for any project on which they work or propose.

   - When deciding on the specifications for this project, we tried to set reasonable goals for what we could accomplish over one semester. Furthermore, as we worked on the project, we tried to set reasonable short-term goals as well.
  
2. Clause 3.10: Ensure adequate testing, debugging, and review of software and related documents on which they work.
   
   - While working on this project, we followed the system of pull requests and code reviews. This way, all of us tested our code before submitting, and then another person reviewed and tested the code before merging. We also used unit tests to ensure proper functionality of some code modules.
  
3. Clause 3.11: Ensure adequate documentation, including significant problems discovered and solutions adopted, for any project on which they work.
   
   - As we came across problems in our application, we added them to our issues on Github. We also posed problems and responded to each other in comments on our pull requests.

4. Clause 6.08: Take responsibility for detecting, correcting, and reporting errors in software and associated documents on which they work.
   
   - As we identified errors in each other's code, and our own code, we made sure to communicate them during our meetings or through text. This allowed us to keep track of the deficiencies in our code and know the limitations of our system.
  
5. Clause 7.04: Review the work of others in an objective, candid, and properly-documented way.
   
   - When we reviewed each other's code, we tried to respect each other's stylistic choices, and only recommend changes in the case of objective errors. We also documented our reviews using comments on pull requests, that way we wouldn't forget any issues we discovered.
  
6. Clause 7.05: Give a fair hearing to the opinions, concerns, or complaints of a colleague.
   
   - When responding to our teammates' critiques of our work, we tried to consider their points before responding. This allowed us to look at our work in a new light, and not simply be defensive.
  
7. Clause 8.01: Further their knowledge of developments in the analysis, specification, design, development, maintenance and testing of software and related documents, together with the management of the development process.

   - As we worked on this project, we tried to research the most modern and effective methods for accomplishing any required tasks. We did our best not to use outdated techniques or bad style.
  
8. Clause 8.03: Improve their ability to produce accurate, informative, and well-written documentation.

   - Over the course of the semester, we got better at recording all of our issues on Github, and documenting any changes we made to our codebase. We put special focus on documenting the proper ways to access any methods or API requests we implemented, since that is the main way others will interract with our code.
  
9. Clause 8.04: Improve their understanding of the software and related documents on which they work and of the environment in which they will be used.

   - As we worked on our app, we became more comfortable and familiar with the tools required for our project. For instance, we became much more familiar with Docker, the main environment in which our system was run.
  
10. Clause 8.06: Improve their knowledge of this Code, its interpretation, and its application to their work.

   - In a lecture devoted to the Code of Ethics, and when working on this document, we studied the Code and considered its application and relevance to our work.
